Foundational Heritage

01.09.1947

THE DAY INDIA ESTABLISHED ITS OWN TIME

“On September 1, 1947, independent India set its clocks to UTC+5:30. It was a statement of sovereignty — that from that day forward, this nation would run on its own time.”

Most luxury watches in the world were born from foreign climates, European yacht clubs, or mountain summits. But the time of Indian daily life is different. It is emotional, elastic, chaotic, precise, funny, sacred, and unmistakably ours. It is the ‘bas paanch minute’, the endless wedding celebrations, the golden hour over red sandstone fortresses, and the breathless tension of a final over.

IST 1947 was founded to turn these lived Indian experiences into modern, serious mechanical and quartz timepieces. We take our horology seriously: 316L stainless steel, anti-reflective sapphire crystal, automatic calibres with open-heart balance wheels, and custom Bregnagari numerals that honour Indian typography.
